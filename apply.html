Build a campaign

Configure it, fund it on chain, and we review it. Your token becomes an extra paycheck brokers can opt into, bought on the open market in small windows every day and released hour by hour after the delay you choose.

Funding does not guarantee approval. Until we approve, every wei you sent can be withdrawn by you, by you alone, at any time. Nothing is spent before approval.
