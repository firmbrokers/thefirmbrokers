01

How to start

Five steps. About ten minutes.

1. Get a wallet

A wallet is an app that holds your coins and your brokers. Phantom or MetaMask both work. Install it, write down your secret phrase on paper, and keep that paper safe. Anyone who sees that phrase can take everything. Never type it into a website.

Minting happens on OpenSea The HR desk sends you to our official OpenSea page, and that is where you mint. Use a normal wallet with ETH on Robinhood Chain — MetaMask, Phantom and Rabby all work. Smart-account wallets, multisigs and OpenSea’s “pay from another chain” option do not work for this mint: the contract only mints to the wallet that signed. We never link any other mint page: a “Firm Brokers mint” that is not on opensea.io is fake.

2. Put some ETH on Robinhood Chain

ETH is the coin used to pay for things here. Robinhood Chain is the network we live on. You need a small amount of ETH on that network to buy a broker and to pay tiny network fees.

3. Get past the front desk

The HR building is locked at first. A guard stands outside it. Walk up to his booth and fill in the short application:

  • Paste in your wallet address
  • Type in your X name — the desk draws your employee card
  • Post the card on X with the text it gives you (the post is the application)
  • Paste the link to your post

Do all four and the guard lets you in. That application is the whitelist. Whitelist buyers mint first, up to 3 brokers per wallet.

The door unlocks for everyone later When the public round opens, the guard steps aside and the form is gone. Anyone can walk in and mint up to 3 brokers per wallet. That is the same 3 for everyone: a whitelist wallet that already took its 3 is done.

4. Mint a broker

Walk to the HR desk. It has one button, MINT ON OPENSEA, which opens our official OpenSea page — the mint happens there. Each broker costs 0.0035 ETH. You see exactly which broker you got right away. No waiting, no reveal day.

5. Hire him

Buying a broker is like hiring nobody yet. He is sitting at home. To put him to work you burn 25,000 $9TO5. After that, he earns every hour.

Two different things ETH buys the broker. $9TO5 puts him to work. You need both. Get $9TO5 →
$9TO5 does not exist yet The token is born on launch day, right before the mint opens. When it is live, a green button appears here and all over the street. That button is the only safe way to buy. If you see a “9TO5” anywhere before then, it is fake. Do not buy it.

03

Burning $9TO5

Burning destroys $9TO5 for ever. It is how a broker is put to work.

Burning sounds dramatic. It is simple. You send $9TO5 to an address that nobody owns and nobody has the key to. The coins stay there for ever. Nobody can ever spend them again.

Nobody receives your burned coins. Not us, not the treasury, not another holder. They leave the supply and never come back.

Why burning exists A broker is free to own and costs $9TO5 to use. Burning is the price of putting him to work, and the only thing that decides what he earns.

04

Hiring your broker

Burn 25,000 $9TO5 and he starts working.

Burning means sending tokens to an address that has no key. They leave circulation for good, and that is the point: every hire makes $9TO5 rarer for everyone still holding it.

Once hired, your broker starts earning at the start of the next hour. He does not earn for the hour he was hired in. That keeps each hour's pay with the brokers who actually worked it.

05

Getting paid

Every hour, working brokers share a pot of money.

Once an hour, all the fees collected are added up into one pot. That pot is split between every broker who was working that hour. Each broker's share depends on his weight, a number that goes up the more $9TO5 you have burned on him.

A broker with double the weight gets double the pay. That is the whole rule.

What you get paid in

You get paid in tokenized stocks (real shares held as tokens) and in USDG, a coin that is worth one dollar. The launch menu: Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Google, Meta, Tesla, Palantir, AMD, GameStop and SpaceX, plus USDG. Until you pick, a broker is paid in USDG.

You choose the mix. You can tell your broker to take all stocks, all dollars, or a blend of up to three things. You can change your mind whenever you want.

Where the pay goes

You pick one of two ways, on each broker's card.

Straight to your wallet. The pay lands in your wallet every hour. There is nothing to do and nothing to claim. New brokers start this way.

Into his vault. The pay waits in a vault that belongs to the broker. You move it to your wallet whenever you like, for free. Only the owner can. One button on the Trading Floor empties every vault at once.

Why anyone picks the vault Pay in the vault belongs to the broker, so it travels with him. Sell a broker with pay inside and the buyer gets that pay too. He is worth more, like selling a piggy bank with the coins still in it.

You hire and promote at the furnace in The Bank. Hire him first. The chain refuses to promote a broker who was never hired, so you cannot waste a burn by doing it backwards.

07

Merging brokers

Turn two or three brokers into one stronger broker.

You can merge brokers you own. One survives and the others are absorbed into him, so the total number of brokers gets smaller.

Merging costs $9TO5, in the same way hiring does. You burn it to do the merge:

  • Two into one: burn 50,000 $9TO5. The two brokers' weights add together, and then the survivor gets a +20% bonus on top.
  • Adding a third: burn another 100,000 $9TO5. The bonus grows to +30%. Three is the most you can merge into one.

Any unclaimed pay inside the destroyed brokers moves into the survivor. Nothing is lost.

08

Legendary brokers

Ten brokers are one-of-a-kind, and they earn 50% more.

Ten brokers were drawn by hand instead of generated. They are called the first ten employees, and each one looks nothing like the other 2,490: different poses, different scenes, broken frames.

A legendary broker earns 50% more than a normal broker who burned the same amount. The bonus stacks on top of promotions and merge bonuses too.

Everyone has the same odds Which broker you get is decided by the blockchain at the moment you buy, from information that only exists once your purchase happens. The draw works the same way for us as for anyone else, and all ten legendaries went into the public pool.

Beyond the ten, rarity comes from traits. Some things are common, like a plain navy suit. Some are very rare, like a gold suit, a golden tie, or laser eyes. Marketplaces work rarity out automatically from the traits.

10

Selling and moving brokers

You can sell any time. Selling stops the broker working.

When a broker moves to a new wallet, he clocks out and stops earning. The new owner burns 25,000 $9TO5 to put him back to work.

What travels with him: his promotions, his merge bonus, and any unclaimed pay in his vault. Earning restarts as soon as someone hires him again.

Where the money actually comes from

Every payday is funded by trading fees on $9TO5.

This is the part most people want to check, so here it is with real numbers.

Every time somebody buys or sells $9TO5, a 3% fee is charged. Most of that fee flows to the project. Then it is split, and the split is written into a contract that stays fixed for everyone, including us.

That 80/20 split lives in a small contract of its own, created at launch, with no owner and no off switch. It stays exactly as it is, for everyone, permanently.

Then it becomes stocks

The payroll money arrives as ETH. Once an hour, a bot swaps it into whatever stocks and dollars the brokers asked for, and hands each broker their share. Anyone can trigger this. The bot just saves people the trouble.

Pay tracks trading. Busy weeks pay more, quiet weeks pay less. It is a real share of real activity rather than a fixed number, which is what lets it keep running without anyone topping it up.

13

Things that are permanent

Three actions cannot be undone. Worth knowing before you click.

  • Burning is permanent. $9TO5 you burn to hire, promote or merge is gone for good. That is the design: every burn makes the token rarer.
  • Merging is permanent. Merged brokers stay merged, and the supply stays smaller.
  • Transfers are final. Like any crypto transfer anywhere, sending to the wrong address is permanent. Checking the address is on you.
14

What you can count on

The important parts are guaranteed by code, and you can check every one.

  • The trading pool is locked in for good. It stays exactly where it is, permanently, for everyone.
  • Payroll keeps its 80% for good. That contract has no owner, so the split simply stays as it is.
  • Your art is safe on IPFS. A file's address is made from the file itself, so your picture is provably the real one.
  • The rules run themselves. After launch we hand back the collection's controls and the code takes over.
  • Payday is always yours to trigger. Any holder can run it directly, whenever they like.
  • One key stays with the team, and here is exactly what it opens. The payroll engine has an operator who can add salary assets and sweep stuck, undelivered funds out of the machine. Pay that has been delivered is yours alone: your wallet and the vaults have no such key.

Every one of these is enforced by code rather than by a promise, and the contracts are listed below so you can read them yourself.
